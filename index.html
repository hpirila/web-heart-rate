How to enable heart rate broadcasting?

Almost every watch or belt uses the same idea: switch it into heart-rate broadcast / sharing mode, keep that screen open, and make sure no other app (phone, gym machine, etc.) is connected to it. Then come back here and click Connect.

Example: Polar Ignite 3
  1. Press the button → Start training → pick any sport
  2. Open Settings on the pre-training screen
  3. Select Share HR with other devices
  4. Select Use watch as heart rate sensor
  5. Keep the watch on that screen, then click Connect here
